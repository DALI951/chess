Pick the name

Three marks, same rules: dark cinema, one red, legible at 16 px, Arabic and English side by side. My honest pick is at the bottom — read all three first.

KERSATكُرسات

Arabic for “lessons”. The app that teaches you, not just the app that lets you play.

KERSAT
كُرسات
lessons · تعلّم
KERSAT
chess · lessons
Reads as
A school. Progress, levels, lessons.
Good
Explains the whole product in one word. The four squares are literally your progress bar, and the red one is “you are here”.
Risk
Narrow. Sounds like homework, not like the place you hang out. Two words in English: “Kersat Chess”.
Domain
kersat / kersat.tn — probably free, definitely not taken.
SHATRANGIشطرنجي

The Arabic word itself, “my chess”. Heritage, warmth, unmistakably local.

SHATRANGI
شطرنجي
chess, in your own language
شطرنجي
chess · community
Reads as
Home. Your chess, your language, your people.
Good
The strongest emotional pull of the three. An eight-pointed star is authentically Tunisian/Maghrebi, not a chess cliché — it is the opposite of every generic knight logo.
Risk
Long + hard to spell. Nobody types “shatrangi” into a search bar. Spoken it is two syllables; typed it is a typo every time.
Domain
shatrangi / shatranji.com — the dot-com is very likely gone, .tn probably not.
DUBESدوباس

Invented, four letters, no meaning to argue about. Pure product energy.

DUBES
دوباس
play smarter
DUBES
chess · ranked
Reads as
A product. Fast, competitive, a bit futuristic.
Good
The mark is a knight’s move drawn as an L with the landing square in red — the one idea nobody else has. Four letters, two syllables, one domain, zero spelling lessons.
Risk
Means nothing. No heritage, no instant meaning. You have to teach the name, and “dubes” means nothing in Arabic or French-Tunisian, so a Tunisian user gets zero context from it.
Domain
dubes / dubes.tn — cheap, and if you ever leave the chess niche the name still works.

My honest pick: SHATRANGI. It is the only one that is yours instead of generic, and the star mark is the only one of the three that could not be mistaken for a template. Yes, it is a mouthful and yes, typing it is a pain — so the fix is shatrangi.tn in the URL and “شطرنجي” on every Arabic surface, which is 90% of your users anyway. If you would rather have zero friction and scale a global brand later, take DUBES. KERSAT is the best lesson name and the worst app name — it boxes you into tutorials before you have a single multiplayer game.
